Create a premium 10-second seamless-loop motion banner for Digimium, a digital subscription storefront. Use the supplied Digimium logo and original service-logo assets as exact identity references.

Canvas: landscape 3:2, 1536×1024, 30 fps. The video will appear in a 340px-wide website advertisement, so the composition must remain clear at small sizes.

Use Digimium's royal blue #1F45B8 background shading softly into brand navy #051650. Keep the white Digimium symbol and exact lowercase “digimium.” wordmark prominent, crisp and stable.

Show exactly these eight service logos, each once: ChatGPT, Gemini, Adobe Photoshop (Ps), Adobe Lightroom (Lr), Evoto, RunningHub, Spotify and Netflix. No Canva or CapCut. Preserve every original logo's shape, lettering and brand colors. Put the logos on premium app tiles with subtle 3D thickness, soft directional lighting and realistic shadows.

Arrange them as a loose, asymmetrical floating composition: varied sizes, heights, depth, spacing and gentle tilts. Use a small irregular cluster and scattered foreground/background tiles. Do not arrange them in a circle, ring, orbit, grid, evenly spaced border or synchronized formation. Keep the main Digimium identity readable with ample breathing room. No cropping or logo collisions.

Animate each tile independently with slow, restrained levitation, slight diagonal drift and tiny rotations. Use different directions, timing and phases. Keep the camera locked and the Digimium lockup steady. No spinning logos, pulsing scale, zooms, camera shake, fast movement, flashing, particles or neon effects. Motion should feel like app tiles suspended naturally in space.

Make the final frame match the first frame in position, velocity, lighting and shadows for a seamless loop with no jump or fade. All logos remain recognizable throughout. No extra text, captions, prices, calls to action, watermarks or audio. The Myanmar description will be added separately as live website text.

Deliver web-ready MP4 (H.264, no audio), plus WebM if supported, and a matching still poster. Target a compact file below 4 MB where visual quality allows. Preserve the full composition when scaled down.
